# The Horn and the Heir

A novel of the Widowed Throne. Outline built on the Save the Cat beat sheet.

## Premise

A hundred years after the Horn of Anointing vanished, a Hornbearer who lost his banner to a merchant's gold goes looking for the heir of the Shepherd's line. He finds a tall, brave young man and is sure he has found a king. The Horn has already chosen the man's thirteen-year-old sister, who keeps sheep and laughs at knights.

## Theme

"The Lord does not see as man sees. Man looks on the outward appearance, but the Lord looks on the heart." (1 Samuel 16:7)

Giants fall to small things. God chooses the least likely, and a man who wants strength has to learn to follow instead of choosing.

## Hero

**Stephanos of the Horn.** Armenian cataphract, about thirty-four, nine years in the Tagma. His count sold the banner Stephanos carried to Leon Goldhand, and Stephanos walked away from the coin. Virtue: Courage. Passion: Anger.

- **Want:** find the heir and the Horn, end the war, and wipe out the shame of the sold banner.
- **Need:** stop measuring by strength, let go of his anger, and trust God to choose.
- **Flaw in action:** he picks the tall heir (Petros) and treats the true heir (Theodora) as a child to protect.

## The four Words of Symeon

Symeon split his last word in four and hid the pieces in the four fallen themes.

1. Waters: "Where the proud one stood, the Horn lay down."
2. Gardens: "It went down with the lamps, and it keeps the door."
3. Heights: "The strong will seek it, and it will not rise for them."
4. Wilderness: "When the least of the Shepherd's house goes down, the Horn will come up."

## Beat sheet

Percentages follow the Save the Cat guide for a novel of about 90,000 words in 26 chapters and a prologue.

| # | Beat | Where | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Opening Image (0 to 1%) | Prologue, Ch 1 | The night of the statue, a hundred years ago: the Horn goes down into the dark. Then the present: Stephanos on the Land Walls at dawn with a trowel in one hand and a spear in the other, angry at the mockers on the hills. |
| 2 | Theme Stated (5%) | Ch 1 | The blind Abba: "Samuel looked at the tallest son too. You are looking for the tallest man in the room, Stephanos." Stephanos hears it and does not understand it. |
| 3 | Setup (1 to 10%) | Ch 1 to 3 | The City, the council split by forged letters, the Sword and Shepherd factions, the Domestikos and Lord Bardas. The save-the-cat moment: Stephanos pulls a mason's boy from under a gibbor's axe. Theophanes arrives at night with Concord knives behind him. The band forms: Kale, Thekla, Lazaros, Sergios. First descent into the Undercroft, where Andronikos' tomb stands empty. |
| 4 | Catalyst (10%) | Ch 4 | In the Drowned Cisterns the band breaks the forgers. Among the Scribes' seized post they find a letter in the hand of the lame lord, twenty years dead. It names a ford and the first frost. Master Scribe Kallinikos escapes, and he has read it. |
| 5 | Debate (10 to 20%) | Ch 5 | Is the letter a forgery? Theophanes swears it is real. The Domestikos orders Stephanos to ride east with Bardas. The Eparch wants the band on the Fog Tithe. The Abba says only the Question. Stephanos wants to be the one who finds the heir. |
| 6 | Break into Two (20%) | Ch 5 end, Ch 6 | Stephanos disobeys the Domestikos and rides for the ford. At Arkadiopolis he kills a gibbor with a sling stone and meets Theodora. "Come in the morning and see." In the morning he sees lion tracks around the camp, and learns the Concord took her brother Petros to the Goldhand's slave block. |
| 7 | B Story (22%) | Ch 6 to 7 | Theodora joins the band because the giants will come back for her. She laughs at Stephanos, asks why he is always angry, and names her sheep after emperors. Stephanos treats her as a ward. She is the theme walking beside him. |
| 8 | Fun and Games (20 to 50%) | Ch 7 to 14 | The promise of the premise: knights, giants, dragons and Holds. The Waters: the fog that whispers debts, Petros on the slave block, the Drowned Mint, the first vigil lamp, the Goldhand transfigured, generosity breaking the Hoard, and mercy (the Goldhand repents like Zacchaeus). The Gardens: the orphans, a fast, the Garden Below, the Mother of Festivals, who refuses mercy. The Heights: the Spring Champion, Petros boasting, the Iron Tax, the Barrow of Gathos. |
| 9 | Midpoint (50%) | Ch 14 | False victory. Anak falls and the Heights are free. Stephanos finds the Sling of Poimen, and it does not fit his hand. It fits Theodora's. The third Word says the strong will not raise the Horn. At the same hour, riders bring news: the Sword faction has crowned Lord Bardas in the Hippodrome, and Nebrodes is marching. |
| 10 | Bad Guys Close In (50 to 75%) | Ch 15 to 18 | A radiant stranger with no shadow tells Petros he is chosen, and sends him to Bardas. Kallinikos writes to Theophanes. The band splits. In the Wilderness the noonday demon empties Stephanos of hope, and Amma Thekla teaches him to pray with his hands. The Noonday King repents at the last. The fourth Word points to "the least." Back in the City, Stephanos chooses the sword: he rides with Bardas and leaves Theodora behind, saying she is a child. |
| 11 | All Is Lost (75%) | Ch 19 | At Melantias, Nebrodes breaks Bardas' host. The stranger vanishes and Bardas dies under the giants. Dove dies. Stephanos' belt of Truth is cold. Theophanes has sold the four Words to Kallinikos for Theodora's safety, and the Concord has taken her and Petros. The whiff of death: the Abba dies in Stephanos' arms, asking the Question. |
| 12 | Dark Night of the Soul (75 to 80%) | Ch 20 | Alone in the roofless Great Church, Stephanos meets the Lion of Judah. Three questions for three failures. "What is that to you? You must follow me." |
| 13 | Break into Three (80%) | Ch 20 end | Stephanos reads the four Words with new eyes. He cannot choose the heir. He can go down with her. |
| 14 | Finale (80 to 99%) | Ch 21 to 25 | **Gathering the team:** Sergios walks into Nebrodes' camp as a madman. Theophanes repents and forges a Concord release order. The band regathers. **Executing the plan:** as Nebrodes storms the walls, the band descends through the Catacombs of the Apostates. **High tower surprise:** at the Gate of Sheol, the Gatekeeper demands a toll, and Kallinikos arrives with Nebrodes' seers to take the Horn. **Dig deep down:** Stephanos stands in the gap and asks the Question; Theophanes speaks Christ's claim to the keys of death. **New plan:** Theodora goes down alone, the least of the Shepherd's house, and the Horn comes up in her hands. In the Great Church the cherub bars Nebrodes, and Theodora kills him with the Shepherd's sling. The Lion names her before the City. |
| 15 | Final Image (99 to 100%) | Ch 26 | Dawn on the Land Walls, mirroring the opening. Stephanos teaches novices the sling instead of cursing the hills. The roof of the Great Church is going up. The Empress Theodora keeps a shepherd's crook by the throne. |
